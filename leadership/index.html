Leadership

From volunteer to financial analyst to chief financial officer at Meri Bhi Aik Khwaish Hai, a student-run nonprofit at Nixor College in Karachi.

Meri Bhi Aik Khwaish Hai (“I too have a wish”) is run entirely by Nixor College students. Over a year and a half I moved from volunteering at relief drives to keeping the organization's books.

  1. Oct 2022 - Feb 2023

    Chief Financial Officer

    Meri Bhi Aik Khwaish Hai

    Promoted after working as the financial analyst. I managed the entity's cash inflows and outflows and its bank account, working with one financial analyst, and put forward economical but effective ways to run our events and projects.

  2. Feb 2022 - Oct 2022

    Financial Analyst

    Meri Bhi Aik Khwaish Hai

    Handled the entity's financial documents and helped arrange events and the materials they needed.

  3. Jul 2021 - Feb 2022

    Volunteer

    Meri Bhi Aik Khwaish Hai

    Worked on short-term relief events for Afghan refugee children, the transgender community and differently-abled people.

What the team did

PKR 1.5M+ raised by our team of twenty students for underprivileged communities.

  • Ran road drives that bought seven hearing aids for people with hearing impairments.
  • Collected more than 1,500 pieces of warm clothing and distributed them to homeless people in Clifton, Karachi.
  • With Nixor Hospital, ran Project Akhuwat: rations, clothes and free medical check-ups for a hundred flood-affected families in Muzaffarabad Colony.
  • After the 2022 floods, visited farmers in Wanki Wasi village in Sindh with moral and financial support.
  • Organized Mere Alfaaz, Mera Andaaz, the first parliamentary debate between Nixor students and hearing-impaired participants.
  • Held pottery and painting sessions with people with Down syndrome, and distributed food parcels to construction workers in DHA-Clifton.
