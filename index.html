About

I'm a senior in Computer Science at LUMS in Lahore. My work sits where machine learning meets reliability: out-of-distribution detection, interpretability with sparse autoencoders, and robust optimization for federated learning.

I'm just as interested in software engineering research, especially software testing, AI for software engineering (AI4SE), software engineering for AI systems (SE4AI), and how agents can take on real work across the development process. My study of build-outcome predictors in CI batching is where the two sides meet.

I've taught in some form since 2020, from one-to-one tutoring to a 300-student operating systems course, and I care about building things that work outside the lab. I'm applying to research master's and PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Build-outcome predictors inside CI batching

Does a better build-outcome predictor actually save more CI runs once it decides which builds a batching workflow skips?

92 to 98% of held-out builds got the same decision from BuildFast and RavenBuild-CR (Cohen's κ 0.72 to 0.84), so the newer predictor's accuracy gains did not turn into CI savings
