Campus Event Discovery and Management Platform

An Android app that puts scattered campus events, RSVPs and organizer tools in one place.

Software Engineering, LUMS, team of five

commits
65 / 117
user stories shipped
15

The problem

Campus events are scattered across group chats and posters, so students miss them and organizers juggle RSVPs and capacity by hand.

My role

I built keyword search, the event-edit and audit-log flows, event display cards, and organizer settings and notifications. I fixed five bugs across event approval, RSVP counts and admin moderation, carried out the final integration and tests, and wrote the Javadoc and sprint backlogs.

The approach

An Android app on Firebase Auth, Firestore and Storage, built in agile sprints with a pull-request workflow, and tested with JUnit unit tests and Espresso UI tests.

The result

A working prototype covering 15 user stories: browsing, search and filters, capacity-aware RSVPs, chat between organizers and students, reminders and organizer statistics, demonstrated in a recorded walkthrough. The repository belongs to the course organization and is private.
